1. Education

My educational journey has been a blend of foundational learning and transformative experiences. At Andinet High School, I developed a solid academic base, where I first discovered my passion for technology and problem-solving. This passion led me to Addis Ababa University, where I delved deeper into the technical world, exploring algorithms, data structures, and the art of building scalable systems. The rigorous environment there challenged me to think critically and sparked my drive to create innovative solutions.

Seeking hands-on, practical skills, I joined Kuraz Bootcamp, a game-changing experience that immersed me in modern software engineering practices. From building full-stack applications to collaborating on real-world projects, I honed my ability to translate ideas into working solutions. Additionally, my time at the Goethe Institute offered a unique opportunity to expand my perspective through cultural exploration, sharpening my communication skills and fostering creativity—qualities that now enrich my technical work.

2. Interests

I have a deep-seated passion for all things technology, and my interests span a broad spectrum. As a software engineer, I’m captivated by designing elegant systems that solve real-world problems. Whether it’s crafting intuitive web applications, building scalable back-end architectures, or architecting robust cloud computing environments, I love creating solutions that are as efficient as they are innovative. Real-time systems, in particular, excite me because they demand precision, speed, and adaptability—qualities that I constantly strive to embody in my work.

Beyond software, I’m fascinated by data science and its ability to turn raw numbers into actionable insights. From developing machine learning models to visualizing trends, I enjoy the process of uncovering stories hidden within data. What ties all my interests together is a love for problem-solving and the thrill of learning something new every day. Technology is ever-evolving, and I’m excited to keep evolving with it, one line of code at a time.
